# 6. Build the camera frame

<p class="pf-step-count">Assembly · Step 6 of 19</p>

<p class="pf-profile-banner" data-guide-device="trimui-smart-pro" data-layout-id="chassis-core-v1"><strong>Compatible DUT:</strong> TrimUI Smart Pro <span>·</span> <code>chassis-core-v1</code></p>

Join the two uprights and two crossbars into the smaller movable rectangle.
Build it flat on the bench so its orientation is easy to see.

## Get these parts

<ul class="pf-part-list">
  <li><span class="pf-key pf-key--black" aria-hidden="true"></span><span><strong>2 × loaded camera-frame uprights</strong><small>From Step 5</small></span></li>
  <li><span class="pf-key pf-key--black" aria-hidden="true"></span><span><strong>2 × loaded 306 mm crossbars</strong><small>Lower and upper</small></span></li>
  <li><span class="pf-key pf-key--black" aria-hidden="true"></span><span><strong>4 × concealed metal L connectors</strong><small>With eight supplied set screws</small></span></li>
  <li><span class="pf-key pf-key--orange" aria-hidden="true"></span><span><strong>4 × keyed camera-frame joint plates</strong><small>Print Batch 03 · one at each upright end</small></span></li>
  <li><span class="pf-key pf-key--magenta" aria-hidden="true"></span><span><strong>4 × M3 × 12 mm screws and wide washers</strong><small>Already serving as handles in the four upright nut bars</small></span></li>
</ul>

## Do this

1. Lay the two uprights parallel with their loaded grooves facing each other.
2. Turn both crossbars so their loaded grooves face the operator side.
3. Insert one concealed L connector at each end of both crossbars.
4. Join the lower and upper crossbars between the uprights.
5. Snug the connector screws, but leave them loose enough that each crossbar
   can still slide up or down later.
6. At every upright end, place one orange joint plate. Its vertical key enters
   the loaded upright groove; its perpendicular key points outward, ready to
   enter an outer depth rail.
7. Attach each plate to its upright with one screw and washer. Leave it loose.
8. Compare opposite sides and corner-to-corner measurements. Adjust until the
   camera frame is rectangular rather than diamond-shaped.

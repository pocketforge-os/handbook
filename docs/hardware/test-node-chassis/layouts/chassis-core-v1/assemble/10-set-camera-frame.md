# 10. Seat and position the camera frame

<p class="pf-step-count">Assembly · Step 10 of 19</p>

<p class="pf-profile-banner" data-guide-device="trimui-smart-pro" data-layout-id="chassis-core-v1"><strong>Compatible DUT:</strong> TrimUI Smart Pro <span>·</span> <code>chassis-core-v1</code></p>

Engage both lower joint plates, then set the camera-frame centerline 75 mm from
the outside of the operator-side rail.

<div class="pf-step-copy" markdown="1">

## Get these parts

<ul class="pf-part-list">
  <li><span class="pf-key pf-key--orange" aria-hidden="true"></span><span><strong>The 2 × lower camera-frame joint plates</strong><small>Already attached loosely to the camera-frame uprights</small></span></li>
  <li><span class="pf-key pf-key--magenta" aria-hidden="true"></span><span><strong>2 × M3 × 12 mm screws and wide washers</strong><small>Already serving as handles in the lower depth-rail nut bars</small></span></li>
  <li><span class="pf-key pf-key--blue" aria-hidden="true"></span><span><strong>Ruler or tape measure</strong><small>Readable to at least 1 mm</small></span></li>
</ul>

## Do this

1. On the left, align the lower orange plate with the inside groove of the left
   lower depth rail.
2. Slide the nearby loose nut bar under the plate hole.
3. Seat both plate keys. The plate must lie flat without being pulled down by a
   screw.
4. Install one screw and washer loosely through the plate into the nut bar.
5. Repeat on the right lower depth rail.
6. Slide the complete camera frame toward or away from the operator until its
   upright centerline measures **75 mm** from the operator-side outside face.
7. Confirm the left and right measurements match.
8. Lightly snug the two lower depth-rail screws.
9. Slide both upper joint plates near the future upper-rail height. Leave them
   loose and visible.

</div>
</div>

<div class="pf-step-check" markdown="1">

**Before you continue:** both lower joint plates lie flat with both keys
engaged, the left and right camera-frame centerlines are 75 mm from the
operator-side outside face, and the two upper plates remain loose for the top
ring.

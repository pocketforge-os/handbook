# 10. Close the outer frame

<p class="pf-step-count">Assembly · Step 10 of 17</p>

<p class="pf-profile-banner" data-guide-device="trimui-smart-pro-s" data-layout-id="chassis-dualbar-v1"><strong>Compatible DUT:</strong> TrimUI Smart Pro S <span>·</span> <code>chassis-dualbar-v1</code></p>

makes it easier to keep the ring level.

## Do this

1. Remove loose tools and fasteners from inside the lower frame.
2. With one person on each side if available, lift the upper ring by its outer
   rails—not by either fixture bar.
3. Keep `WIDTH-O-U` over the operator side and `WIDTH-D-U` over the device
   side.
4. Align all four connector sockets directly above their matching labeled
   posts.
5. Lower the ring in the blue-arrow direction, keeping it level so all four
   post ends enter together.
6. If one corner resists, lift the ring clear and realign it. Do not pull a
   corner into place with connector screws.
7. Seat the ring until every upper rail is flush with its adjacent post face.
8. Engage the four upper post fasteners only finger-snug. Final tightening
   happens after the diagonal check.

# 8. Build the upper ring flat

<p class="pf-step-count">Assembly · Step 8 of 18</p>

<p class="pf-profile-banner" data-layout-id="chassis-dualbar-v1"><strong>Compatible topology:</strong> Current registered device packs <span>·</span> <code>chassis-dualbar-v1</code></p>

Build the complete upper rectangle on the bench. Do not place it on the posts
until its own fixture bar is installed in the next step.

## Get these parts

<ul class="pf-part-list">
  <li><span class="pf-part-tag pf-part-tag--aluminum">Width</span><span><strong><code>WIDTH-O-U</code> and <code>WIDTH-D-U</code></strong><small>Two preloaded 306 mm upper width rails</small></span></li>
  <li><span class="pf-part-tag pf-part-tag--aluminum">Depth</span><span><strong><code>DEPTH-L-U</code> and <code>DEPTH-R-U</code></strong><small>Two preloaded 318 mm upper depth rails</small></span></li>
  <li><span class="pf-part-tag pf-part-tag--metal">Metal</span><span><strong>4 × three-way end connectors</strong><small>The remaining cap-flush corner connectors</small></span></li>
  <li><span class="pf-part-tag pf-part-tag--tool">Tool</span><span><strong>Square and matching hex driver</strong><small>Leave all four corners adjustable</small></span></li>
</ul>

## Do this

1. On a second flat area, put `WIDTH-O-U` nearest the operator edge and
   `WIDTH-D-U` parallel on the far device side.
2. Put `DEPTH-L-U` on the left and `DEPTH-R-U` on the right.
3. Turn `WIDTH-O-U` so its two placard bars face outward toward the operator.
4. Turn `WIDTH-D-U` so its two holder bars face inward toward the operator.
5. Turn both depth rails so their blue-tagged parked-bar grooves face inward.
6. Fit one three-way connector at each corner with its post socket facing
   down—the direction the post will enter when the ring is lifted.
7. Engage all supplied fasteners, push every rail end flush, and leave the
   four corners finger-snug.
8. Check the flat rectangle with the square, but do not apply final torque.

# 11. Square and tighten the frame

<p class="pf-step-count">Assembly · Step 11 of 18</p>

<p class="pf-profile-banner" data-layout-id="chassis-dualbar-v1"><strong>Compatible topology:</strong> Current registered device packs <span>·</span> <code>chassis-dualbar-v1</code></p>

Match both pairs of corner-to-corner diagonals before applying final torque.
The maximum difference within either pair is **2 mm**.

## Get these parts

<ul class="pf-part-list">
  <li><span class="pf-part-tag pf-part-tag--assembled">Assembled</span><span><strong>Complete loose chassis</strong><small>Both fixture bars installed at the same 75 mm datum</small></span></li>
  <li><span class="pf-part-tag pf-part-tag--tool">Measure</span><span><strong>Tape measure and machinist square</strong><small>Use the same metal reference points for each diagonal</small></span></li>
  <li><span class="pf-part-tag pf-part-tag--tool">Tighten</span><span><strong>Connector hex drivers</strong><small>Tighten opposite corners in a cross pattern</small></span></li>
</ul>

## Do this

1. Measure both corner-to-corner diagonals of the lower rectangle from the
   same metal reference points.
2. Shift the loose frame until the lower diagonal pair differs by **no more
   than 2 mm**.
3. Repeat the two measurements on the upper rectangle and bring that pair
   within 2 mm.
4. Put the machinist square against each post in both directions. Correct any
   lean while the outer joints remain loose.
5. Tighten one lower corner, then its opposite corner. Tighten the other pair
   in the same cross pattern.
6. Repeat the cross pattern on the upper corners, increasing torque in small,
   even passes.
7. Recheck both diagonal pairs and all four posts after every pass. Loosen and
   correct the frame if tightening moves it outside tolerance.
8. Confirm both fixture-bar centerlines still read 75 mm at left and right.
9. Put the chassis on a flat surface and check that it does not rock.

</div>

<div class="pf-step-check" markdown="1">

**Before you continue:** lower and upper diagonal pairs each differ by no more
than 2 mm, all four posts are plumb, every outer joint is flush and tight, both
fixture bars remain at 75 mm, and the chassis does not rock.

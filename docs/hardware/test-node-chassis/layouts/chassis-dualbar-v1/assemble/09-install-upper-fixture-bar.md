# 9. Install the upper fixture bar

<p class="pf-step-count">Assembly · Step 9 of 18</p>

<p class="pf-profile-banner" data-layout-id="chassis-dualbar-v1"><strong>Compatible topology:</strong> Current registered device packs <span>·</span> <code>chassis-dualbar-v1</code></p>

Add the second continuous support while the upper ring is separate and easy to
reach. Match the lower bar's 75 mm operator-side datum.

## Do this

1. Keep `WIDTH-O-U` at the operator edge of the flat upper ring.
2. Place the bar between `DEPTH-L-U` and `DEPTH-R-U` so it runs left to right.
3. Put one printed joint plate **below** each end. Seat its perpendicular keys
   in the fixture bar's downward slot and the matching depth rail's downward
   slot. Align both holes with their preloaded active joint bars.
4. Turn its loaded groove and both active link bars toward the operator.
5. Set the fixture-bar centerline **75 mm from the operator-side outside
   plane** at both ends.
6. Square the bar to both depth rails and tighten all four joint-plate screws
   evenly. Keep each broad plate flat and both keys fully seated.
7. Confirm the operator-facing active link bars and parked bar still slide and
   remain clear of the printed joints.
8. Recheck 75 mm on the left and right before lifting the ring.

# 9. Install the upper fixture bar

<p class="pf-step-count">Assembly · Step 9 of 17</p>

<p class="pf-profile-banner" data-guide-device="trimui-smart-pro-s" data-layout-id="chassis-dualbar-v1"><strong>Compatible DUT:</strong> TrimUI Smart Pro S <span>·</span> <code>chassis-dualbar-v1</code></p>

Add the second continuous support while the upper ring is separate and easy to
reach. Match the lower bar's 75 mm operator-side datum.

## Do this

1. Keep `WIDTH-O-U` at the operator edge of the flat upper ring.
2. Put one concealed L-connector at each end of `FIXTURE-U`, using the lower
   grooves of the fixture bar and upper depth rails.
3. Place the bar between `DEPTH-L-U` and `DEPTH-R-U` so it runs left to right.
4. Turn its loaded groove and both active link bars toward the operator.
5. Set the fixture-bar centerline **75 mm from the operator-side outside
   plane** at both ends.
6. Square the bar to both depth rails and tighten the two L-connectors evenly.
7. Confirm the active and parked channel bars still slide and remain clear of
   the metal connectors.
8. Recheck 75 mm on the left and right before lifting the ring.

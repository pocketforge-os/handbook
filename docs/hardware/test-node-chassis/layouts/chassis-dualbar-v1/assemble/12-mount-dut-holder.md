# 12. Mount the Smart Pro S holder

<p class="pf-step-count">Assembly · Step 12 of 17</p>

<p class="pf-profile-banner" data-layout-id="chassis-dualbar-v1"><strong>Compatible topology:</strong> Current registered device packs <span>·</span> <code>chassis-dualbar-v1</code></p>

Attach the verified holder to the two device-side width rails. The pair marked
for the upper rail stays above the holder; the lower pair stays below it.

!!! warning "Keep one verified device pack together"
    Use the holder, J-hooks, links, and joint hardware from the same Smart Pro S
    device pack. Do not mix visually similar parts from another device.

## Do this

1. Put the two `TOP` links above the holder and the two `BOTTOM` links below
   it, following the verified pack markings.
2. Loosely attach each link's slotted end to its matching holder corner with
   one verified joint-hardware set.
3. Turn the holder so its hooks and the future DUT screen face the operator.
4. Present the assembly to the operator-facing grooves of `WIDTH-D-U` and
   `WIDTH-D-L`.
5. Move the two active channel bars in each rail under the keyed rail-side link
   holes.
6. Fasten the upper links to `WIDTH-D-U` and the lower links to `WIDTH-D-L`
   using the four M3 × 12 mm rail screws and wide washers.
7. Center the holder at **X = 173 mm**, measured from either outside side face
   of the 346 mm-wide chassis.
8. Adjust the four holder slots until all links lie flat, then tighten the
   eight joints evenly by hand.
9. Sight across the upper and lower chassis stack planes. Every carrier link
   must end **1 mm inside** its corresponding aluminum plane; no printed edge
   may protrude beyond the frame.
10. Place the fully unpowered Smart Pro S in the holder for a fit check. Confirm
   every hook clears its controls, ports, speakers, vents, triggers, and
   display, then remove it if later work could drop hardware onto it.

unpowered DUT fits without blocked controls or forced contact.

# 14. Add the node placard

<p class="pf-step-count">Assembly · Step 14 of 17</p>

<p class="pf-profile-banner" data-guide-device="trimui-smart-pro-s" data-layout-id="chassis-dualbar-v1"><strong>Compatible DUT:</strong> TrimUI Smart Pro S <span>·</span> <code>chassis-dualbar-v1</code></p>

Mount the reusable holder below the upper operator width rail, then slide the
device-name cartridge in from the right.

## Do this

1. Find the two active channel bars in the groove facing outward toward the
   operator on `WIDTH-O-U`.
2. Move one keyed spacer under each active bar.
3. Hang one riser below each spacer and fasten both risers loosely to the rail
   using the existing M3 × 12 mm screws and wide washers.
4. Hold the placard holder between the risers with its countersunk holes facing
   the name cartridge.
5. Join the holder to the two risers with the flat-head M3 screws and ordinary
   metal nuts.
6. Center and level the holder, then tighten all four fasteners evenly.
7. Slide the name cartridge in from the right until its built-in stop reaches
   the holder.
8. Pull it partway back out to prove the fixture board does not block service,
   then seat it again.

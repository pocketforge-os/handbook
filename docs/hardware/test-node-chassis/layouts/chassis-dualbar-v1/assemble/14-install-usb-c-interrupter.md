# 14. Install the dual USB-C interrupter holder

<p class="pf-step-count">Assembly · Step 14 of 18</p>

<p class="pf-profile-banner" data-layout-id="chassis-dualbar-v1"><strong>Compatible topology:</strong> Current registered device packs <span>·</span> <code>chassis-dualbar-v1</code></p>

Mount both USB-C interrupter boards in one printed holder on the upper fixture
bar. Keep the ports facing the operator and place the holder exactly 5.0 mm to
the right of the left fixture-board link.

## Do this

1. Confirm the fixture board, interrupter boards, USB hub, programmable supply,
   relay supply, power strip, and every USB cable are de-energized and
   disconnected.
2. Seat one interrupter board in each pocket with both USB-C receptacles facing
   the operator side.
3. Retain each board with two M1.7 × 6 mm self-tapping screws. Tighten only
   until the boards cannot move; do not crush the circuit boards.
4. Insert two M3 drop-in T-nuts into the operator-facing groove of
   `FIXTURE-U` and loosely fasten the holder to them.
5. Slide the holder immediately to the right of the left fixture-board link.
   Set an exact **5.0 mm edge-to-edge gap** between the link and holder.
6. Keep the holder square to the rail and tighten both M3 screws evenly.
7. Test access with loose, unpowered USB-C plugs. Each plug must insert and
   withdraw without contacting the link, fixture board, rail, or other port;
   remove both plugs again.

fixture-board link, both ports face the operator, and neither port nor cable
path collides with adjacent hardware. The fixture remains unpowered.

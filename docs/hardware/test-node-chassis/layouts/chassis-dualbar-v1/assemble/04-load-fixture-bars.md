# 4. Load both fixture bars

<p class="pf-step-count">Assembly · Step 4 of 17</p>

<p class="pf-profile-banner" data-guide-device="trimui-smart-pro-s" data-layout-id="chassis-dualbar-v1"><strong>Compatible DUT:</strong> TrimUI Smart Pro S <span>·</span> <code>chassis-dualbar-v1</code></p>

Put two active link positions and one blue-tagged parked spare into each
continuous fixture bar, then account for all 20 channel bars.

## Get these parts

<ul class="pf-part-list">
  <li><span class="pf-part-tag pf-part-tag--aluminum">Aluminum</span><span><strong>2 × continuous 306 mm fixture bars</strong><small><code>FIXTURE-L</code> and <code>FIXTURE-U</code>; no joint interrupts either span</small></span></li>
  <li><span class="pf-part-tag pf-part-tag--printed">Printed</span><span><strong>6 × compact M3 channel bars</strong><small>Four active link positions and two parked replacements</small></span></li>
  <li><span class="pf-part-tag pf-part-tag--fastener">M3</span><span><strong>6 × M3 × 12 mm screws and wide washers</strong><small>Loose temporary handles</small></span></li>
  <li><span class="pf-part-tag pf-part-tag--tool">Mark</span><span><strong>Blue removable tape</strong><small>One parked-spare tag per fixture bar</small></span></li>
</ul>

## Do this

1. Place `FIXTURE-L` and `FIXTURE-U` side by side with their labels visible.
2. Choose the groove on each rail that will face the operator.
3. Insert **2 active bars** into `FIXTURE-L`. Spread them left and right so
   they can later reach the fixture-board slots.
4. Insert **1 parked bar** into the same groove, tag its screw blue, and leave
   it near a cut end.
5. Repeat the same **2 active + 1 parked** preload in `FIXTURE-U`.
6. Leave all six handles loose and confirm every bar slides without binding.
7. Audit the complete preload: **6 active width + 4 active right-depth + 4
   parked depth + 4 active fixture + 2 parked fixture = 20**.

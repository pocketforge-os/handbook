# 4. Load both fixture bars

<p class="pf-step-count">Assembly · Step 4 of 17</p>

<p class="pf-profile-banner" data-layout-id="chassis-dualbar-v1"><strong>Compatible topology:</strong> Current registered device packs <span>·</span> <code>chassis-dualbar-v1</code></p>

Put two active link positions, two active printed-joint positions, and one
blue-tagged parked spare into each continuous fixture bar, then account for
all 28 channel bars.

## Get these parts

<ul class="pf-part-list">
  <li><span class="pf-part-tag pf-part-tag--aluminum">Aluminum</span><span><strong>2 × continuous 306 mm fixture bars</strong><small><code>FIXTURE-L</code> and <code>FIXTURE-U</code>; no joint interrupts either span</small></span></li>
  <li><span class="pf-part-tag pf-part-tag--printed">Printed</span><span><strong>10 × compact M3 channel bars</strong><small>Four active link positions, four active printed-joint positions, and two parked replacements</small></span></li>

## Do this

1. Place `FIXTURE-L` and `FIXTURE-U` side by side with their labels visible.
2. Choose the groove on each rail that will face the operator.
3. Insert **2 active link bars + 1 parked bar** into the operator-facing groove
   of `FIXTURE-L`. Spread the active pair toward the fixture-board slots, tag
   only the parked screw blue, and leave the spare near a cut end.
4. Insert **2 active joint bars** near the ends of `FIXTURE-L`'s upward groove.
5. Repeat with **2 active links + 1 parked** in `FIXTURE-U`'s operator-facing
   groove and **2 active joints** in its downward groove.
6. Leave all ten handles loose and confirm every bar slides without binding.
7. Audit the complete preload: **6 active width + 8 active depth + 4 parked
   depth + 8 active fixture + 2 parked fixture = 28**.

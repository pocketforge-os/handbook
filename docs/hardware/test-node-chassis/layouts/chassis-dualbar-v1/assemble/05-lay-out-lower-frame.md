# 5. Lay out the lower frame

<p class="pf-step-count">Assembly · Step 5 of 18</p>

<p class="pf-profile-banner" data-layout-id="chassis-dualbar-v1"><strong>Compatible topology:</strong> Current registered device packs <span>·</span> <code>chassis-dualbar-v1</code></p>

Resolve operator, device, left, and right while all four lower rails are still
flat on the bench. Join the corners loosely and leave the post sockets open.

## Do this

1. Stand at one edge of a flat bench and call it the **operator side**.
2. Put `WIDTH-O-L` nearest you and `WIDTH-D-L` parallel to it on the far
   device side.
3. Put `DEPTH-L-L` on your left and `DEPTH-R-L` on your right between the
   width rails.
4. Turn `WIDTH-D-L` so its two active bars face the operator, into the frame.
5. Turn both depth rails so their loaded grooves face inward. The five bars in
   `DEPTH-R-L` therefore face left, toward the frame center.
6. At each corner, fit one three-way connector so the width rail butts one
   post face and the depth rail butts the adjacent face. Neither rail crosses
   the other.
7. Keep the connector's post socket open upward. Engage every supplied
   fastener, then leave all four corners only finger-snug.
8. Push each rail end flush against its connector and check the rectangle with
   the square without applying final torque.

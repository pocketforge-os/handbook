# 16. Mount the unplugged power strip

<p class="pf-step-count">Assembly · Step 16 of 18</p>

<p class="pf-profile-banner" data-layout-id="chassis-dualbar-v1"><strong>Compatible topology:</strong> Current registered device packs <span>·</span> <code>chassis-dualbar-v1</code></p>

Run the strip front-to-back on two printed blocks inside the lower
operator-right depth rail. Keep its mains plug visibly disconnected.

## Do this

1. Put the strip's disconnected mains plug where you can see it.
2. Find `DEPTH-R-L`, the lower rail on your right when standing at the
   operator side. Leave its one blue-tagged parked bar alone.
3. Move the four active channel bars in the inward groove into two pairs, one
   pair for each printed block.
4. Loosely fasten both blocks to the inside face of the rail with the four M3
   × 12 mm screws and wide washers.
5. Measure the real strip's wall-mount keyhole positions. Slide the two blocks
   front or back until their printed pilots match those positions.
6. If the supplied screws need larger pilots, remove the blocks from the
   chassis and drill **only the printed ABS** to the screws' root diameter.
7. Drive the four supplied wall screws into the printed blocks. Leave each
   head at the strip's keyhole-retention gap.
8. Hang the unplugged strip on those four heads with its long axis running
   front-to-back inside the frame.
9. Tighten the four block-to-rail M3 screws. Confirm the switch, outlets, cord,
   and removal direction remain accessible.

!!! danger "Never drill the strip or aluminum"
    Printed pilot holes are drill starts in replaceable ABS. Do not drill,
    pierce, or drive a wall screw into the power-strip enclosure or any
    aluminum rail.

**Before you continue:** the mains plug is still disconnected, the strip runs
front-to-back inside `DEPTH-R-L`, four M3 fasteners clamp two printed blocks,
all four supplied screws enter printed ABS only, and the fixture board and
service controls remain clear.

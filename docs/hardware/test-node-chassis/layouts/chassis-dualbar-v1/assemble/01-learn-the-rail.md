# 1. Learn the rail and channel bar

<p class="pf-step-count">Assembly · Step 1 of 17</p>

<p class="pf-profile-banner" data-layout-id="chassis-dualbar-v1"><strong>Compatible topology:</strong> Current registered device packs <span>·</span> <code>chassis-dualbar-v1</code></p>

Practice the hidden sliding interface once before loading any labeled frame
rail. The compact printed carrier is **18 mm end to end**.

!!! warning "Measure the carrier first"
    Do not use a carrier that is longer than 18 mm or one that binds in the
    selected aluminum. Reprint or correct the fit before assembly; never force
    a carrier with a hammer.

## Get these parts

<ul class="pf-part-list">
  <li><span class="pf-part-tag pf-part-tag--printed">Printed</span><span><strong>1 × compact 18 mm M3 channel bar</strong><small>One ordinary M3 nut already seated in its pocket</small></span></li>
  <li><span class="pf-part-tag pf-part-tag--fastener">M3</span><span><strong>1 × M3 × 12 mm screw and wide washer</strong><small>Temporary removable handle</small></span></li>
  <li><span class="pf-part-tag pf-part-tag--aluminum">Aluminum</span><span><strong>1 × aluminum offcut or unlabeled practice rail</strong><small>Do not use a labeled frame rail yet</small></span></li>
</ul>

## Do this

1. Confirm the carrier measures **18 mm** end to end and its ordinary M3 nut
   lies flat in the printed pocket.
2. Put the wide washer on the screw, then turn the screw into the nut by only
   one turn. Keep the screw loose so it works as a handle.
3. Find the rail's open **cut end**. Do not try to push the bar through the
   narrow long groove.
4. Hold the carrier with its broad printed face toward the groove and the
   metal nut toward the rail center.
5. Feed the carrier through the cut end in the blue-arrow direction.
6. Slide it end to end. Pull the handle gently toward the groove and confirm
   the aluminum lips retain the bar.
7. Slide the carrier back out and keep its screw and washer with it.

</div>

<div class="pf-step-check" markdown="1">

**Before you continue:** the 18 mm carrier slides freely, stays behind the
groove lips when pulled gently, and comes back out through the cut end. Deburr
the aluminum and repeat if it catches.

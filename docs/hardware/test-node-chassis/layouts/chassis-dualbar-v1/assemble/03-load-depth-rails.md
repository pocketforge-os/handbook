# 3. Load the four depth rails

<p class="pf-step-count">Assembly · Step 3 of 17</p>

<p class="pf-profile-banner" data-guide-device="trimui-smart-pro-s" data-layout-id="chassis-dualbar-v1"><strong>Compatible DUT:</strong> TrimUI Smart Pro S <span>·</span> <code>chassis-dualbar-v1</code></p>

Load the four active power-strip positions into the lower operator-right depth
rail, then put one blue-tagged parked spare in every depth rail.

## Get these parts

<ul class="pf-part-list">
  <li><span class="pf-part-tag pf-part-tag--aluminum">Aluminum</span><span><strong>4 × 318 mm labeled depth rails</strong><small><code>DEPTH-L-L</code>, <code>DEPTH-R-L</code>, <code>DEPTH-L-U</code>, and <code>DEPTH-R-U</code></small></span></li>
  <li><span class="pf-part-tag pf-part-tag--printed">Printed</span><span><strong>8 × compact M3 channel bars</strong><small>Four active power-strip bars and four parked replacements</small></span></li>
  <li><span class="pf-part-tag pf-part-tag--fastener">M3</span><span><strong>8 × M3 × 12 mm screws and wide washers</strong><small>Loose temporary handles</small></span></li>
  <li><span class="pf-part-tag pf-part-tag--tool">Mark</span><span><strong>Blue removable tape</strong><small>Tag only the four parked-spare screws</small></span></li>
</ul>

<div class="pf-picture-key" role="group" aria-label="Depth-rail preload picture annotations">
  <p class="pf-picture-key__title">Read the picture</p>
  <ul>
    <li><span class="pf-cue pf-cue--orange">Orange bars</span><span>Four untagged bars on <code>DEPTH-R-L</code> become the power-strip mounts.</span></li>
    <li><span class="pf-cue pf-cue--blue">Blue tags</span><span>One tagged bar per rail is a parked replacement and attaches to nothing now.</span></li>
    <li><span class="pf-cue pf-cue--silver">Silver rails</span><span>The loaded groove on each rail must face the finished frame interior.</span></li>
  </ul>
</div>

## Do this

1. Lay out the four depth rails by tape code and identify `DEPTH-R-L`: the
   lower right rail when viewed from the operator side.
2. Choose the groove on `DEPTH-R-L` that will face the frame interior. Insert
   **4 active bars** and leave their screws untagged.
3. Insert one additional bar into that same groove. Wrap blue tape around its
   screw to identify it as a parked spare.
4. In the future inward-facing groove of `DEPTH-L-L`, insert **1 parked bar**
   and tag its screw blue.
5. Repeat with **1 parked bar** in `DEPTH-L-U` and **1 parked bar** in
   `DEPTH-R-U`.
6. Move each parked bar near a cut end but keep it fully behind the groove
   lips. Leave its handle only finger-snug.
7. Count **four active + four parked = eight depth-rail bars**.

</div>

one blue-tagged spare; each other depth rail contains one blue-tagged spare;

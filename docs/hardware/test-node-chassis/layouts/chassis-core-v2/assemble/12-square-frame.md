# 12. Square and tighten the frame

<p class="pf-step-count">Assembly · Step 12 of 19</p>

<p class="pf-profile-banner" data-guide-device="trimui-smart-pro" data-layout-id="chassis-core-v2"><strong>Compatible DUT:</strong> TrimUI Smart Pro <span>·</span> <code>chassis-core-v2</code></p>

Attach the two upper camera-frame joints, then remove any diamond-shaped skew
from both outer rectangles before final tightening.

## Get these parts

<ul class="pf-part-list">
  <li><span class="pf-key pf-key--magenta" aria-hidden="true"></span><span><strong>2 × M3 × 12 mm screws and wide washers</strong><small>Already serving as handles in the upper depth-rail nut bars</small></span></li>
  <li><span class="pf-key pf-key--blue" aria-hidden="true"></span><span><strong>Tape measure and machinist square</strong><small>For paired diagonals and post plumb</small></span></li>
  <li><span class="pf-key pf-key--black" aria-hidden="true"></span><span><strong>Hex drivers for both connector types</strong><small>Tighten by hand; do not use an impact driver</small></span></li>
</ul>

## Do this · attach the upper joints

1. Seat the left upper joint plate in both the camera-frame upright and left
   upper depth-rail grooves.
2. Move the loose nut bar under its hole and install one screw and washer.
3. Repeat on the right. Leave both screws loose until the frame is square.

## Square the chassis

1. Confirm the outside envelope is approximately **346 mm wide × 358 mm deep ×
   368 mm tall**.
2. Measure the two corner-to-corner diagonals of the lower rectangle.
3. Shift the still-loose frame until those values differ by no more than 2 mm.
4. Repeat on the upper rectangle.
5. Use the square to make all four posts plumb.
6. Tighten opposite outer corners in a cross pattern.
7. Recheck both diagonal pairs after every pass.
8. Tighten all eight camera-frame joint screws by hand.

</div>
</div>

<div class="pf-step-check" markdown="1">

**Before you continue:** both lower diagonals match within 2 mm, both upper
diagonals match within 2 mm, all posts are plumb, every horizontal rail remains
flush, and the chassis does not rock on a flat surface.

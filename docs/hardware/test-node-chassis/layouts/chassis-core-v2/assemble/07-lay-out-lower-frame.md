# 7. Lay out the lower outer frame

<p class="pf-step-count">Assembly · Step 7 of 19</p>

<p class="pf-profile-banner" data-guide-device="trimui-smart-pro" data-layout-id="chassis-core-v2"><strong>Compatible DUT:</strong> TrimUI Smart Pro <span>·</span> <code>chassis-core-v2</code></p>

Resolve all four directions before adding a connector. The rectangle stays flat
and loose in this step.

## Get these parts

<ul class="pf-part-list">
  <li><span class="pf-key pf-key--black" aria-hidden="true"></span><span><strong>1 × operator lower width rail</strong><small>306 mm · <code>WIDTH-O-L</code> · four loaded bars</small></span></li>
  <li><span class="pf-key pf-key--black" aria-hidden="true"></span><span><strong>1 × device lower width rail</strong><small>306 mm · <code>WIDTH-D-L</code> · two loaded bars</small></span></li>
  <li><span class="pf-key pf-key--black" aria-hidden="true"></span><span><strong>1 × left lower depth rail</strong><small>318 mm · <code>DEPTH-L-L</code> · one active bar and one blue spare</small></span></li>
  <li><span class="pf-key pf-key--black" aria-hidden="true"></span><span><strong>1 × right lower depth rail</strong><small>318 mm · <code>DEPTH-R-L</code> · one active bar and one blue spare</small></span></li>
</ul>

## Do this

1. Clear a flat work surface larger than the finished 346 × 358 mm rectangle.
2. Mark the edge nearest you **OPERATOR** with removable tape.
3. Place the operator lower rail along that edge.
4. Place the device lower rail parallel to it on the far side.
5. Put the left and right lower depth rails between them as shown.
6. Confirm every tape label is on the correct side before turning any rail to
   its final face.
7. Turn the rails so the operator lower rail's four-bar groove faces into the
   rectangle, the device lower rail's two-bar groove faces into the rectangle,
   and each depth rail's loaded groove faces into the rectangle.
8. Check that all six loose use-now bars remain present and that the two blue
   spares remain lightly parked.

</div>

<div class="pf-step-check" markdown="1">

**Before you continue:** the tape labels and loaded grooves match the picture.
The width and depth rails meet only at their ends; they do not overlap. Do not
install a connector until this orientation is correct.

# 3. Load the four depth rails

<p class="pf-step-count">Assembly · Step 3 of 19</p>

The **depth rails** run from the operator side to the device side. Pick up
**eight identical compact 18 mm orange sliding nut carriers** from Print Batch
01. There is no separate blue printed part.

Each rail receives two of those orange bars. One stays loose for a camera-frame
joint later in this build. The other is lightly parked for possible future
service; a small piece of blue removable tape marks that bar's screw.

!!! note "What is the parked replacement for?"
    It attaches to **nothing during this build**. The rail's open cut ends will
    soon be blocked by connectors, so adding a nut bar later would require
    reopening the frame. Parking one now leaves a ready M3 attachment point
    inside the groove. If it is ever needed, loosen its screw and slide it into
    position. Otherwise, leave it parked.

!!! warning "Why the 18 mm length matters here"
    The carrier must stay inside the 20 mm landing beneath each camera-frame
    upright joint. The superseded 30 mm carrier overhangs that landing and can
    catch the top/bottom joint. Stop if any short carrier is longer than 18 mm;
    use the corrected Batch 01 part or the
    [existing-part correction](../print.md#check-the-short-carrier-length).

!!! note "The blocks identify a groove, not an insertion route"
    Insert every orange bar through an **open cut end**. The small markers hover
    outside the long face only to show which groove is loaded. The narrow blue
    stripe is a role label, not another component.

## Match all four rails

| Rail | Inside groove faces | Load |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `DEPTH-L-L` | Right, toward center | 2 identical orange bars: 1 untagged + 1 blue-tape-tagged |
| `DEPTH-R-L` | Left, toward center | 2 identical orange bars: 1 untagged + 1 blue-tape-tagged |
| `DEPTH-L-U` | Right, toward center | 2 identical orange bars: 1 untagged + 1 blue-tape-tagged |
| `DEPTH-R-U` | Left, toward center | 2 identical orange bars: 1 untagged + 1 blue-tape-tagged |

## Do this

1. Lay out the lower pair and upper pair exactly as pictured, with
   **OPERATOR** nearest you and every tape label readable.
2. Put a wide washer on each of the eight screws. Thread one screw into each
   short orange bar by **one turn** so it acts as a loose handle.
3. Starting with `DEPTH-L-L`, slide **two identical short orange bars** into
   the right-facing inside groove.
4. Repeat on `DEPTH-R-L`, using its left-facing inside groove.
5. Repeat the same mirrored pair on `DEPTH-L-U` and `DEPTH-R-U`.
6. On every rail, choose the bar nearest the future **DEVICE / WALL** end as
   the parked replacement. Leave enough empty rail beyond it for the metal
   corner-connector tongue.
7. Gently tighten that parked bar's screw only until its washer stops the bar
   from sliding or rattling. It carries no load.
8. Mark the parked screw-and-washer assembly with a small piece of blue
   removable tape. Keep the hex socket accessible.
9. Leave the other, untagged orange bar loose on its screw handle. It will meet
   a camera-frame joint later.

<div class="pf-step-check" markdown="1">

**Before you continue:** every depth rail contains exactly **two identical
orange bars in its inside groove**—one loose and untagged, plus one lightly
parked at the device end with blue tape on its screw. That is **8 orange bars
total, with blue tape on 4 of them**.

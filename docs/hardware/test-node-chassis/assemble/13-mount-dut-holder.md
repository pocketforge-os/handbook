# 13. Mount the DUT holder

<p class="pf-step-count">Assembly · Step 13 of 19</p>

Attach the device-specific holder to the two device-side width rails. The short
links go on top and the long links go on the bottom.

## Get these parts

<ul class="pf-part-list">
  <li><span class="pf-key pf-key--black" aria-hidden="true"></span><span><strong>1 × device-specific DUT holder</strong><small>TrimUI Smart Pro family holder in the example build</small></span></li>
  <li><span class="pf-key pf-key--orange" aria-hidden="true"></span><span><strong>2 × 97.5 mm upper links</strong><small>Print Batch 03 · the shorter pair</small></span></li>
  <li><span class="pf-key pf-key--orange" aria-hidden="true"></span><span><strong>2 × 114.5 mm lower links</strong><small>Print Batch 03 · the longer pair</small></span></li>
  <li><span class="pf-key pf-key--magenta" aria-hidden="true"></span><span><strong>8 × M3 × 12 mm screws and wide washers</strong><small>Four already in the rail nut bars; four for the holder joints</small></span></li>
  <li><span class="pf-key pf-key--magenta" aria-hidden="true"></span><span><strong>4 × ordinary M3 hex nuts</strong><small>One at each link-to-holder joint</small></span></li>
</ul>

## Do this

1. Put the two short links above the holder and the two long links below it.
2. Loosely attach each link's slotted end to the matching holder corner with
   one screw, washer, and ordinary M3 nut.
3. Turn the holder so its hooks and future DUT screen face the operator.
4. Present the assembly to the operator-facing grooves of the two device-side
   width rails.
5. Align each link's round keyed end with one preloaded nut bar.
6. Fasten the short links to the upper rail and the long links to the lower
   rail.
7. Center the holder at **X = 173 mm**, measured from either outside side face
   of the 346 mm-wide chassis.
8. Adjust the four slots until the holder lies flat.
9. Tighten the four rail screws and four holder-joint screws evenly by hand.

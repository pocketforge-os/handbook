# 14. Mount the electronics plate

<p class="pf-step-count">Assembly · Step 14 of 19</p>

Attach the electronics fixture plate to the two camera-frame crossbars. One
5 mm keyed spacer belongs at every corner.

## Get these parts

<ul class="pf-part-list">
  <li><span class="pf-key pf-key--black" aria-hidden="true"></span><span><strong>1 × electronics fixture plate</strong><small>With the Logitech C270 mount in the standard example</small></span></li>
  <li><span class="pf-key pf-key--orange" aria-hidden="true"></span><span><strong>4 × 5 mm keyed plate spacers</strong><small>Print Batch 03</small></span></li>
  <li><span class="pf-key pf-key--magenta" aria-hidden="true"></span><span><strong>4 × M3 × 12 mm screws and wide washers</strong><small>Already serving as handles in the four crossbar nut bars</small></span></li>
</ul>

## Do this

1. Turn the plate so its component side faces the operator.
2. Confirm the C270 lens faces away from the operator and toward the DUT holder.
3. Put one orange spacer behind each of the plate's four corner slots.
4. Seat each spacer key in the operator-facing groove of its crossbar.
5. Move the four loose nut bars under the four plate slots.
6. Install one screw and washer at each corner. Leave all four loose.
7. Slide the two crossbars vertically until every spacer lies flat and the
   plate is not twisted.
8. Center the plate left to right.
9. Tighten the four screws evenly by hand.
10. Recheck that the camera-frame centerline remains 75 mm from the
    operator-side outside face.

Do not route or connect power and USB cables yet.

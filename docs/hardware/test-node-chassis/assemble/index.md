# Choose a device before assembly

This former unscoped assembly entrance cannot determine which rails and
printed parts are on your bench. It now stops before Step 1 so the exact
device carrier and qualification gate are selected before the common frame.

[Choose the exact DUT and open its build sheet](../index.md).

Every current device sheet routes to the 17-step continuous-bar chapter.
Historical gantry chapters remain available only as records of earlier
qualified builds. Future chassis sizes get their own namespaced sequence here
without changing existing builds.

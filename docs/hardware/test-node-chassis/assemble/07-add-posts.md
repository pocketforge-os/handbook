# 7. Add the four posts

<p class="pf-step-count">Assembly · Step 7 of 17</p>

Raise the outer frame by seating one labeled 360 mm post at each lower corner.
Keep the joints adjustable and leave every post top open.

## Do this

1. Keep the same operator-side viewpoint used in Step 5.
2. Put `POST-OL` into the operator-left lower connector and `POST-OR` into the
   operator-right connector.
3. Put `POST-DL` into the device-left lower connector and `POST-DR` into the
   device-right connector.
4. Press every post fully down until its faces meet the connector and adjacent
   rail ends without a visible gap.
5. Snug the lower connector fasteners only enough that each post stands by
   itself.
6. Check each post against the square in both directions. Shift the loose
   lower rectangle rather than forcing a leaning post.
7. Confirm the open cut end at the top of every post remains unobstructed.

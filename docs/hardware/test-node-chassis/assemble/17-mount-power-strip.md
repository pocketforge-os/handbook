# 17. Mount the unplugged power strip

<p class="pf-step-count">Assembly · Step 17 of 19</p>

Attach two printed blocks to the inside of the lower operator-side rail, then
hang the unplugged strip on its own wall-mount screw heads.

## Do this

1. Put the strip's disconnected mains plug where you can see it.
2. Find the four loose nut bars in the inside groove of the lower operator-side
   width rail.
3. Remove the four temporary M3 × 12 mm screw-and-washer handles from those
   bars and return them to the hardware bin.
4. Loosely mount both printed blocks with the four M3 × 30 mm flat-head screws.
5. Measure the real strip's wall-mount keyhole spacing.
6. Slide the blocks left or right until their pilot holes match that spacing.
7. If the supplied wall screws need larger pilots, remove the blocks and drill
   only the printed ABS to the screws' root diameter.
8. Drive the four supplied screws into the printed blocks. Leave the heads at
   the strip's keyhole-retention gap.
9. Hang the unplugged strip on those heads.
10. Tighten the four block-to-rail screws.

!!! danger "Never drill the power strip or aluminum"
    The 2 mm printed holes are drill starts in replaceable ABS. Do not drill,
    pierce, or drive a screw into the strip enclosure or an aluminum rail.

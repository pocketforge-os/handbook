# 16. Add the node placard

<p class="pf-step-count">Assembly · Step 16 of 19</p>

Mount the reusable holder below the upper operator-side rail, then slide the
device-name cartridge in from the right.

## Do this

1. Find the two loose nut bars in the groove facing the operator on the upper
   operator-side width rail.
2. Put one keyed spacer against the rail at each nut bar.
3. Hang one riser below each spacer and fasten both risers loosely to the rail.
4. Hold the placard holder between the risers with its countersunk holes facing
   the name cartridge.
5. Use the two flat-head M3 screws and two ordinary M3 nuts at the
   holder-to-riser joints.
6. Center and level the holder, then tighten all four fasteners.
7. Slide the name cartridge in from the right in the blue-arrow direction.
8. Stop when the cartridge's built-in end stop reaches the holder.

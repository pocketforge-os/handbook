# 13. Mount the populated fixture board

<p class="pf-step-count">Assembly · Step 13 of 17</p>

Join the unpowered populated fixture board to both continuous support bars with
four identical links. Use both upper slots and both lower slots.

## Do this

1. Confirm the fixture board, camera, DUT, programmable supply, relay supply,
   USB power, and power strip are all de-energized.
2. Turn the board so its components face the operator and the C270 lens points
   toward the DUT holder.
3. At the upper left, seat one link's 16 × 6.43 mm key in the operator-facing
   groove of `FIXTURE-U`. Move an active channel bar under its rail-side hole
   and clamp it with the existing M3 × 12 mm screw and wide washer.
4. Put the link below the board's upper-left slot. Clamp through the slot with
   a through-fastener, one wide washer on each side, and a metal locknut.
5. Repeat the same upper connection at the upper-right slot.
6. At the lower left, seat a third link in the operator-facing groove of
   `FIXTURE-L`. The link rises from the bar and sits above the board's
   lower-left slot. Clamp both its rail and plate joints.
7. Repeat the same lower connection at the lower-right slot.
8. Leave all four plate joints just loose enough to remove board twist. Center
   the board left to right, seat all four keys fully, then tighten evenly.
9. Inspect every component, connector, and cable corridor. No link, rail,
   washer, nut, or screw may touch one.

# 5. Load the camera-frame rails

<p class="pf-step-count">Assembly · Step 5 of 19</p>

Load the final 10 identical short orange sliding nut bars into the two completed
**spliced uprights from Step 4** and two camera-frame crossbars. Keep each
upright's orange splice collar installed. Two bars will be tagged and parked as
replacements, completing the full inventory of 28.

!!! note "The markers identify a groove, not an insertion route"
    Every orange bar enters through an **open cut end**. The markers hover
    beside the loaded long face so you can see the exact groove and count. A
    narrow blue stripe is a tape label, not another component.

## Match all four rails

| Rail | Groove faces in operator view | Load |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Left upright | Right, toward other upright | 2 untagged orange bars |
| Right upright | Left, toward other upright | 2 untagged orange bars |
| `GANTRY-CROSS-U` | Operator | 3 orange bars; blue tape on the one parked at far left |
| `GANTRY-CROSS-L` | Operator | 3 orange bars; blue tape on the one parked at far right |

## Load the two uprights

1. Set the completed left and right uprights in the operator view shown.
2. Slide **two orange use-now bars** into the right-facing groove of the left
   upright.
3. Slide **two orange use-now bars** into the left-facing groove of the right
   upright.
4. Leave all four upright bars loose on their screw handles.

## Load the two crossbars

1. Turn both crossbars so the groove that will face the operator is up.
2. In `GANTRY-CROSS-U`, load **three identical orange bars**. Lightly retain
   the far-left bar, then mark its screw with blue tape as pictured.
3. In `GANTRY-CROSS-L`, load **three identical orange bars**. Lightly retain
   the far-right bar, then mark its screw with blue tape as pictured.
4. The two tagged bars attach to nothing during this build. Leave the other
   four crossbar bars loose.

## Count all short bars

- Width rails from Step 2: **10 use-now**.
- Depth rails from Step 3: **4 use-now + 4 tagged parked replacements**.
- Camera frame in this step: **8 use-now + 2 tagged parked replacements**.
- Grand total: **22 use-now + 6 parked replacements = 28 identical orange
  bars**.

<div class="pf-step-check" markdown="1">

**Before you continue:** count all **28 short orange sliding nut bars** now.
Six have blue tape on their screws and are lightly parked; the other 22 remain
movable. A missing bar is easy to fix now and requires disassembly after the
rail ends close. The camera-frame group in front of you contains exactly **10
orange bars, with blue tape on 2 of them**.

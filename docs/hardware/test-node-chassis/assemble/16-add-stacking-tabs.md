# 16. Add the stacking tabs

<p class="pf-step-count">Assembly · Step 16 of 17</p>

Install two narrow registration guides at every upper corner. Their lower
fasteners attach to this chassis; the upper round holes stay empty unless a
second chassis is actually seated above it.

## Do this

1. At one upper corner, hold one tab flat against each of the two exterior post
   faces. The tapered ends and single round holes point up.
2. Insert two M5 drop-in T-nuts into the open groove behind one tab's long
   slots.
3. Add a flat washer to each M5 × 10 mm screw, pass the screws through the
   slots, and engage the nuts by two or three turns.
4. Center the 18 mm tab on the 20 mm post face, leaving about 1 mm of aluminum
   visible along each long edge.
5. Set the lower end **60 mm below the chassis's top aluminum surface**. The
   tapered tip then extends **32 mm above that surface**, and the upper
   round-hole center sits **17 mm above it**. This is 7 mm above the former
   hole position so it clears the delivered metal corner intrusion.
6. Snug both lower screws only until the tab cannot slide by hand. Do not crush
   the printed part.
7. Install the perpendicular tab on the other exterior face, then repeat at
   the remaining three corners.
8. Count **2 tabs × 4 corners = 8 tabs** and **2 lower fasteners per tab = 16
   lower M5 sets**. Leave all eight upper round holes empty.
9. Store the eight upper locking sets with the chassis. If a second chassis is
   later installed, lower it without forcing until aluminum touches aluminum
   at every corner, then use one upper locking set per tab.

!!! danger "Provisional two-chassis limit"
    Stack no more than two populated chassis until a load test establishes a
    rated count. The printed tabs provide lateral registration only; aluminum
    carries the vertical load. Use a stable surface and restrain the pair
    against tipping.
